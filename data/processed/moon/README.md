# Moon processed products

LOLA LDEM_4 is the only retained active Moon dataset. Its real JP2 source was integrity-verified, decoded, and processed to `lola/ldem_4_height_m.npy` with a metadata sidecar and three explicitly unvalidated display previews. It is not independently validated or map-ready. No other lunar products are acquired. Preserve source lineage, units, NoData policy, and checksums for any future retained product.
