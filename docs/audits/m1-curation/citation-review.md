# Citation-to-claim review

The M1 citation register is embedded per record in `data/curation/reference-curation-v1.json` and summarized in the [site report](site-curation-report.md). Each attached citation includes title, responsible author/organization, year when available, DOI/stable URL, source type, the narrow claim/dimension it supports, geographic scope, and limitations. Missing publication metadata is left null or explicitly unstated.

## Evidence interpretation

Original atlas citations remain historical leads until the cited source, locality, and claim are checked individually. The M0 report already flags inferred mineral claims for some atlas sites, the ATA precipitation discrepancy, and the absence of site-specific process evidence. Stored provider responses are evidence of a returned product, not proof of correct site identity or scientific representativeness. Missing, not retrieved, failed, and absent are not interchangeable.
